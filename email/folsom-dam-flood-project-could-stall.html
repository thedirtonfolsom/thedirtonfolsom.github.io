Folsom Dam flood project could stall in January

Work to strengthen Folsom Dam against big storms may stop in January because federal money hasn't come through, CBS13 reported on Nov. 24.

The nearly $500 million project raises the dam's dikes and adds new bulkheads to the tops of all eight of its metal gates. That lets the gates hold back about three more feet of water during heavy rain. Work began in 2019, but only one of the eight gates has been finished. The bulkheads for the other seven are already built and sitting at the site.

The backstory: The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers runs the project. A state flood official told CBS13 the Corps needed more funding by the end of August and didn't get it. About $300 million has been spent so far, according to a member of the Central Valley Flood Protection Board. The Sacramento Area Flood Control Agency is lobbying in Washington for the rest. One gate is out of service for the work, but officials don't expect that to affect flood operations this winter.

Why it matters: The project is meant to protect more than half a million people in the Sacramento region downstream on the American River. Officials warned that a pause could add millions of dollars to the final cost.

Source: CBS13

Explainer: Why Folsom Dam matters downstream

Folsom Dam holds back Folsom Lake on the American River. The dam is about 70 years old and is the backbone of flood protection along the river as it runs through Rancho Cordova and Sacramento. How much water it can hold back in a big storm decides how much flows downstream.

Source: CBS13

2

Big 5 is closing its East Bidwell store

Big 5 Sporting Goods is closing its Folsom store at 606 E. Bidwell St. in Model Plaza. "Store Closing" banners are up and a liquidation sale is underway. No final closing date has been announced.

The backstory: Property records show the store's lease ends in early 2027. The California chain, founded in 1955, was taken private in October 2025 after losing money and has been closing stores in several states this year. It hasn't announced closing the whole chain, and its Citrus Heights, Roseville and Rancho Cordova stores are still open.

Why it matters: No new tenant has been named for the space. What fills it will be a sign of how much demand there is for older retail space north of Highway 50 while new shopping centers keep opening in Folsom Ranch.

Source: Folsom Times

3

A late-night bar is coming to El Dorado Hills Town Center

A new bar called The Double Standard plans to open as soon as March 2027 at 4359 Town Center Blvd., Suite 110, in El Dorado Hills Town Center. It takes the roughly 1,400-square-foot space left by the Barrel and Bliss wine bar, which closed for good earlier this month.

The 21-and-older bar plans to be open Wednesday through Sunday, until 1 a.m. most nights, with a full bar and cold food like charcuterie. The owners, Nick Dedier and Alexa Hazelton, also run Native + Nomad and Milestone in Town Center and Mom & Pop Chicken Shop in Cameron Park. They told reporters there is demand for later hours from service workers and Bay Area transplants.

Why it matters: A Town Center space that went dark this month already has a new tenant lined up. The owners are betting that El Dorado Hills wants later nights out, even as they acknowledge people are drinking less overall.

Sources: Sacramento Business Journal · What Now Sacramento

Emblem Empire Ranch Apartments: Quarterra Multifamily plans 180 rental apartments at Empire Ranch Road and Iron Point Road, near Russell Ranch Elementary on the north side of Highway 50. The plan uses 7.55 acres of a 19.25-acre site. The other 11.69 acres would go to the city and is set aside for a future Highway 50 interchange and westbound off-ramp, to be built later by others. The city filed a state notice in April saying the apartments are covered by earlier environmental studies, so no new review is needed. No construction date has been posted. State CEQA filing
