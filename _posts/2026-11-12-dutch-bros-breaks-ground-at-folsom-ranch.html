Dutch Bros breaks ground at Southpointe, Folsom Ranch's newest shopping center

Construction started the second week of November on a Dutch Bros drive-thru on East Bidwell Street, the newest tenant at Southpointe at Folsom Ranch.

The 986-square-foot coffee shop will have two drive-thru lanes that can stack up to 20 cars, plus walk-up windows. When the lease was announced in October 2025, the opening was expected in spring 2026, so the project is running behind that timeline. No new opening date has been announced.

Why it matters: Southpointe is one of the first retail centers serving the thousands of new homes south of Highway 50. Each new tenant means fewer trips across the freeway to shop.

Sources: Sacramento Business Journal via Yahoo · LRE & Co lease announcement

Explainer: What is Folsom Ranch?

Folsom Ranch, officially the Folsom Plan Area, is the city's newest community: about 3,520 acres south of Highway 50, bounded by White Rock Road, Prairie City Road and the El Dorado County line. Folsom voters approved annexing the land with Measure W in 2004. The city plans about 11,000 homes there, and at least 30% of the land must stay open space. Neighborhoods include Broadstone Estates, Mangini Ranch, Russell Ranch and Folsom Heights.

Source: City of Folsom

2

Three EDH road projects get an update

El Dorado County transportation staff briefed the El Dorado Hills Area Planning Advisory Committee on Nov. 11. Where things stand:

  • White Rock Road widening (planning): from two lanes to four between Post Street and just south of Silva Valley Parkway, the stretch that connects Folsom Ranch with south El Dorado Hills.
  • Bass Lake Road and Bridlewood Drive roundabout (planning).
  • Country Club Drive extension (preliminary design): a new link from Silva Valley Parkway to Tong Road.

Why it matters: Thousands of homes are approved or proposed on both sides of the county line, including Alder Creek, Creekside Village and Marble Valley. These roads decide how that traffic moves.

Source: EDH APAC meeting, Nov. 11

3

Heritage Village Park lights are coming down

The El Dorado Hills Community Services District approved a roughly $76,000 contract to remove the 50-foot court lights at Heritage Village Park, off Carson Crossing Drive.

The backstory: The lights went up for the park's tennis and pickleball courts, next to the Heritage 55-and-older community. About 400 nearby residents said the lights lit up their homes. The district limited park hours to dawn to dusk, but the lights stayed because removal was expensive. Residents formed a nonprofit and sued the district and developer Lennar in 2022. This removal comes out of the settlement.

Sources: KCRA · CBS13 · Village Life

Explainer: What is Serrano?

El Dorado Hills' largest master-planned community: about 3,500 acres of gated neighborhoods north of Highway 50, in development since 1989. It's centered on the private Serrano Country Club and its 18-hole Robert Trent Jones Jr. golf course, with about 17 miles of trails. Few vacant lots remain, so most activity now is resales and custom builds.
